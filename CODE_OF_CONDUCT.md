# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors, users and maintainers are expected to participate in a respectful,
constructive and inclusive technical community. Disagreement about architecture
is welcome; personal attacks, harassment and discriminatory behavior are not.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code and design decisions with concrete evidence and reproducible cases.
- Be considerate of people with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect others' time, privacy and intellectual property.
- Credit the work of others, and help newcomers reproduce issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Targeted harassment, insults, intimidation or discriminatory comments.
- Publication of private communications, secrets or personally identifying data.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or abusive behavior in issues and reviews.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove harmful content, lock discussions, decline
contributions or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and,
where appropriate, explained privately to the affected parties.

Report concerns privately through a contact channel listed on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must instead use the private reporting
process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include personal or
sensitive information in public issues.
